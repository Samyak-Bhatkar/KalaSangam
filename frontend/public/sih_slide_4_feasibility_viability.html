🛡️ Security, Trust & Statutory Compliance
DPDP Act 2023 Aligned • Artisan IP Guard
🔒
DPDP Act 2023 Aligned Data Minimization Artisan spoken voice recordings and raw camera inputs are processed ephemerally and purged immediately following text extraction, strictly retaining only necessary product catalog details with explicit consent.
🔑
Encrypted Storage & Protected Transmission Artisan personal identifiers, telephone numbers, and bank disbursement records are protected by strong transport-layer encryption (TLS 1.3) and encrypted databases with strict role-based access isolation.
🔏
Tamper-Evident Product Authenticity Seals Exported catalog imagery and digital certificates incorporate verifiable digital marks to protect traditional artisans from unauthorized image scraping, counterfeiting, or deceptive commercial re-use.
🎯
Craft Honesty Dispute Mitigation Mechanism Designed to reduce buyer disputes and costly return-shipping exposure by allowing artisans to proactively flag natural handmade variations (kiln firing marks, weave nuances) as proof of authenticity rather than defects.
⚖️ Field Risk Analysis & Operational Safeguards
Proactive Failure Mode Protections
RISK 1 Low / Zero Rural Internet Connectivity in Remote Craft Villages
SAFEGUARD Offline-First Local Storage: Captures and drafts buffer securely on the device, automatically syncing to the central queue once a network signal is re-established.
RISK 2 Voice / Speech Misrecognition in Heavy Regional Dialects & Workshop Noise
SAFEGUARD Human-in-the-Loop Review Queue: Ambiguous or low-confidence voice transcriptions route to trusted local SHG / cluster coordinator dashboards for rapid visual verification.
RISK 3 Artisan Distrust or Reluctance Towards Automated AI Price Calculations
SAFEGUARD Mandatory Manual Override Authority: The artisan holds absolute veto control at all times; no listing is ever finalized or exported without explicit artisan audio confirmation and final consent.
