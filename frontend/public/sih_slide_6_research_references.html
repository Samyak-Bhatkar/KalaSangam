⚖️ Legal & Statutory Framework 4 References
Minimum Wages Act, 1948 & Code on Wages ↗ Wage Floor
Ministry of Labour & Employment, Govt. of India • Notified State Schedules
Role in ShilpSetu: Provides statutory legal basis for Module 3 (Fair Price Advisor). Hard-clamps artisan labor cost to official state minimum wage schedules (referenced UP semi-skilled baseline ₹43.75/hr up to ₹120/hr skilled craft benchmark), guaranteeing pricing algorithms can never underbid human subsistence.
Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 ↗ Data Privacy
Act No. 22 of 2023, Gazette of India • Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY)
Role in ShilpSetu: Governs Slide 4 security architecture. Strictly adheres to Sections 4–6 lawful consent mandates through spoken vernacular audio prompts, zero third-party telemetry, ephemeral voice buffer purging, and AES-256 data-at-rest encryption for artisan phone numbers and bank records.
Geographical Indications of Goods Act, 1999 ↗ Authenticity
Act No. 48 of 1999 • Controller General of Patents, Designs & Trade Marks (DPIIT)
Role in ShilpSetu: Backs Slide 4's craft authenticity tags and digital provenance marking. Associates verified artisan listings with recognized GI clusters (e.g. Gorakhpur Terracotta, Bankura Pottery) and embeds tamper-evident metadata seals to protect traditional handcrafts against factory replicas.
