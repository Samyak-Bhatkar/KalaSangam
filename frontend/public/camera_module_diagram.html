📐 Tilt & Angle Level

Reads device gamma/beta roll & pitch from DeviceOrientation API.

Threshold: ±3.5° Level

Classifies: Eye-level (<20°), Angled (20°-60°), Flat-lay (>60°).

💡 Luminance Check

Calculates mean pixel luminance across the RGB color space.

Y = 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B

Flags underexposure (<75) in dim workshops before capture.

🎯 Saliency & Framing

Calculates subject bounding contour area over total frame area.

Target: 60% – 85% area

Prompts artisan to move closer if subject is lost in background.

📳 Motion & Jitter

Computes frame-to-frame pixel differential matrix.

Inhibits on hand tremor

Eliminates blurry photos caused by physical camera shaking.

⚡ 1. SHA-256 LRU Cache

Computes SHA-256 hash of incoming image payload.

5ms instant hit bypass

🧠 2. Tiered Neural Cutout

Salient object segmentation isolating craft with alpha feathering.

  • • Studio Tier: birefnet-general / bria-rmbg
  • • Fast Fallback: u2net / u2netp
  • • Removes cluttered beds, unplastered walls & tools.

🌡️ 3. 6500K Daylight Balancing

OpenCV Gray-World color constancy algorithm.

cv2.cvtColor + Gray-World Normalizer

Transforms 2700K dingy incandescent yellow light into clean 6500K calibrated studio daylight.

📐 4. 10% Breathing Margin & Centering

Auto-crops to the craft's exact alpha mask bounding box, scales symmetrically, and centers it on a 1:1 canvas with standardized 10% breathing margins required by ONDC and Amazon.

🌑 5. Natural Contact Drop Shadow Synthesis

Scans lowest craft edge pixels to find ground contact points. Synthesizes a natural, diffused elliptical contact shadow using Pillow Gaussian blur (sigma = 15) so the craft looks grounded rather than a floating cutout.
