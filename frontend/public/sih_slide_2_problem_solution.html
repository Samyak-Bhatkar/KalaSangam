⚠️ Section 1: The Core Obstacles • Why Marginalized Artisans Cannot Access Digital Commerce
Direct PS 26090 Grounding (MoSJE)
❌
Low Digital Literacy & Regional Language Barriers Artisans cannot navigate complex, English-centric, text-dense seller registration portals or digital forms.
❌
Dim Workshop Photography & Lack of Cataloging Skills Rural workshops lack studio lighting and clean backgrounds, resulting in blurry photos rejected by modern buyers.
❌
Information Asymmetry & Predatory Middleman Exploitation Lacking fair pricing knowledge and market benchmarks, artisans fall into distress selling far below their labor cost.
❌
Severe Dependency on Periodic Physical Exhibitions Sales spike briefly during annual fairs (Shilp Samagam, Surajkund Mela, Dilli Haat) but vanish once the exhibitions conclude.
👥 Who Is Affected • Target Beneficiaries MoSJE Schemes
🏛️
Target Beneficiaries: Traditional craft micro-entrepreneurs, artisans, and weavers supported under NBCFDC & NSFDC (MoSJE).
📱
Smartphone Owners: Possess touchscreen devices but struggle with typing, complex English interfaces, and angle framing.
📞
Feature Phone Owners: Elderly master craftsmen relying solely on basic ₹800 keypad mobiles without mobile internet.
🎯
Design Mandate: True inclusion requires zero text typing and end-to-end access across both device tiers.
💡 Section 2: Proposed Solution • Autonomous Virtual Business Manager
Elevator Pitch Line
ShilpSetu AI is an autonomous virtual business manager that bridges traditional craftsmanship to national digital commerce — enabling marginalized artisans to independently create professional catalogs, receive guaranteed living-wage pricing, and prepare marketplace-ready listings for open commerce rails using only their natural spoken voice and phone.
✓ Zero Text Typing: 100% voice and visual cues ✓ Dual-Tier Ingress: Works on touchscreens & basic ₹800 keypad phones ✓ Middleman-Free: Generates marketplace-ready export formats
⚡ Section 3: Key Innovations • Five Pillars Solving the Specific Artisan Barriers
Human Benefit Framing (Engineering Proved in Slide 3)
Vision Engine 01
📷 AI Photo Studio
Solves: Cluttered Workshop Photos
✕ Before: Photos rejected due to dim light & clutter.
✓ After: Clean white backdrop; true craft texture kept.
Turns raw snapshots taken in dim rural rooms into marketplace-ready white-background showcases — without expensive cameras, lighting setups, or editing skills.
Speech & NLP 02
🎙️ Story & Catalog Writer
Solves: Text & Language Barrier
✕ Before: Can't type in English or fill seller forms.
✓ After: Speak native dialect; AI writes story & tags.
Enables illiterate artisans to simply speak in regional dialects about craft heritage — automatically generating rich storytelling and standardized bilingual listings.
Living Wage 03
⚖️ Fair Price Advisor
Solves: Distress Exploitation
✕ Before: Middlemen force distress sales below cost.
✓ After: Hourly wage floor + material costs protected.
Guarantees artisans are never underpaid by computing a protected living-wage price floor based on verified labor hours and raw materials, shielding from predatory haggling.
Telephony Ingress 04
📞 Voice Line (कला-वाणी)
Solves: Zero-Smartphone Divide
✕ Before: Elderly weavers locked out with no phone.
✓ After: Simple phone call from ₹800 keypad mobile.
Allows elderly master craftsmen without smartphones or internet to onboard, catalog items, and verify orders through a simple toll-free phone call from any basic keypad mobile.
Open Commerce 05
🌐 One-Tap Marketplace
Solves: Seasonal Fair Dependency
✕ Before: Incomes dry up once annual fairs conclude.
✓ After: Marketplace-ready formats for ONDC & GeM.
Generates marketplace-ready listing formats for ONDC and GeM channels, positioning artisans for continuous 365-day digital commerce beyond seasonal exhibition dependencies.
FULL EXPANSIONS MoSJE: Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment • NBCFDC: National Backward Classes Finance & Development Corporation • NSFDC: National Scheduled Castes Finance & Development Corporation • ONDC: Open Network for Digital Commerce • GeM: Government e-Marketplace
