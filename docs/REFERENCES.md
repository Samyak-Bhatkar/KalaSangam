# ShilpSetu AI (शिल्पसेतु AI) — Official References & Technical Citations

**Client Project**: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE), Department of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India  
**Target Beneficiaries**: Marginalized Artisans, Weavers, and Craftspeople (NBCFDC & NSFDC)  
**System**: Autonomous AI-Driven Smart Cataloging, Living-Wage Pricing & Sovereign Commerce Linkage (ONDC & GeM)

---

## 1. Government Frameworks & Statutory Mandates

1. **Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE)**
   - *Description*: Central Government Ministry responsible for welfare, empowerment, and term-loan financing for SC/ST/OBC artisans and micro-entrepreneurs.
   - *Official Portal*: [https://socialjustice.gov.in/](https://socialjustice.gov.in/)
   - *PM-DAKSH Portal*: [https://pmdaksh.dosje.gov.in/](https://pmdaksh.dosje.gov.in/)

2. **National Backward Classes Finance & Development Corporation (NBCFDC)**
   - *Description*: Apex corporation under MoSJE implementing artisan loan schemes (*Shilp Sampada*, *Mahila Samriddhi*) and sponsoring exhibitions (*Shilp Samagam*).
   - *Official Portal*: [https://nbcfdc.gov.in/](https://nbcfdc.gov.in/)
   - *Schemes Guide*: [https://nbcfdc.gov.in/schemes/](https://nbcfdc.gov.in/schemes/)

3. **The Code on Wages, 2019 (Act No. 29 of 2019)**
   - *Description*: Gazette of India enactment establishing the legal basis for the National Floor Level Minimum Wage and statutory skilled artisan labor rates (₹120/hr standard).
   - *Official Gazette (PDF)*: [https://labour.gov.in/sites/default/files/the_code_on_wages_2019.pdf](https://labour.gov.in/sites/default/files/the_code_on_wages_2019.pdf)

4. **GeM (Government e-Marketplace) — Public Procurement Portal**
   - *Description*: National public procurement platform enabling direct institutional government purchases under the Saras Collection and ODOP initiatives (+15% statutory margin).
   - *Official Portal*: [https://gem.gov.in/](https://gem.gov.in/)
   - *One District One Product (ODOP)*: [https://gem.gov.in/odop](https://gem.gov.in/odop)

5. **ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce)**

   - *Official Portal*: [https://ondc.org/](https://ondc.org/)
   - *Developer Guide & Participant Sandbox*: [https://ondc.org/developer-guide/](https://ondc.org/developer-guide/)

6. **Beckn Protocol Core Specifications (v1.2.0)**
   - *Description*: Open-source decentralized transaction protocol governing discoverability, catalog broadcast, order lifecycle, and fulfillment schemas.
   - *Official Specification*: [https://becknprotocol.io/](https://becknprotocol.io/)
   - *GitHub Specifications*: [https://github.com/beckn/protocol-specifications](https://github.com/beckn/protocol-specifications)

## 4. Computer Vision, Steganography & Media Synthesis

9. **U^2-Net: Salient Object Detection (Rembg Background Engine)**
   - *Description*: Two-level nested U-structure deep network for high-resolution salient object detection and pristine background isolation of physical craft photos.
   - *ArXiv Research Paper (Qin et al., 2020)*: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.09007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.09007)
   - *Rembg Open-Source Package*: [https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg](https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg)

10. **SciPy 2D Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) Watermarking**
    - *Description*: Frequency-domain 64-bit invisible steganographic watermark embedded into the luminance ($Y$) channel to authenticate Geographical Indication (GI) tags.
    - *SciPy DCT Documentation*: [https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.fftpack.dct.html](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.fftpack.dct.html)
    - *IEEE Reference on Robust DCT Watermarking*: [https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/736141](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/736141)

11. **MoviePy & Microsoft Edge-TTS Video Synthesis**
    - *Description*: Automated video compositing engine rendering 15-second 9:16 vertical reels with Ken Burns animation and neural Indic voiceovers.
    - *MoviePy Documentation*: [https://zulko.github.io/moviepy/](https://zulko.github.io/moviepy/)
    - *Edge-TTS Python Library*: [https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts)

12. **n8n Workflow Automation Mesh & WhatsApp Cloud API**
    - *Description*: Asynchronous webhook event orchestration mesh running Bargain Guard negotiation and live MoSJE portal analytics synchronization.
    - *n8n Documentation*: [https://docs.n8n.io/](https://docs.n8n.io/)
    - *Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API*: [https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/)
